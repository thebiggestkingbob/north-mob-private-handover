# NORTH MOB page-by-page creative blueprint
All numbered photographs refer to the supplied contact sheet and `asset-inventory.csv`. Proposed only: final cropping and selection must be previewed and approved.

HOME: review group photos 15 and 21 as opening still/fallback; review MOB TIES video for suitability as hero background. Small logo, minimal nav, immersive film transitions.
MUSIC: use official H4B cover only when separately supplied. Editorial album layouts and compact tracklists; persistent mini-player always visible.
VISUALZ: build featured video, YouTube-style discovery and photography. Review photo groups 05, 09, 11, 13, 25; validate content before tagging. Gallery image interactions, metadata, full-screen lightbox.
THE MOB: inspect portrait images and H4B ID-card images in contact sheet; require confirmed mapping before assigning names. Individual media and music.
LIVE: do not mistake MOB TIES editorial photography for confirmed performance photography. Add actual event flyers and verified gig data later.
BUSINESS AS USUAL: formal group photo 15 or 21; elegant minimal adaptive enquiry form, no unnecessary input borders.
SHOP: actual merchandise product photos and pricing are still required. Build functionality, but do not invent products.
STUDIO: private utilitarian CMS matching brand, prioritizing ease of publishing and secure operations.

Global: immersive page-to-page transitions, media-first layouts, responsive mobile variants, motion fallback and accessibility. Avoid heavyweight effects that delay first paint.
